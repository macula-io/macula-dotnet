Report the last candidate failure when a later pass finds none

Once a direct-dial candidate has failed before sending, the call now
throws the most recent candidate failure. A later pass whose query finds
no candidate, or fails, no longer replaces it with ProcedureNotAdvertised,
NoTrustedAdvertisement or a content lookup error. Until a candidate has
been tried, the reason none qualified is still what the call throws.

Found live: a get_direct whose dial reused the resolver session's identity
got that session replaced by the station, so the next lookup failed and
hid the trust violation that the dial itself had reported.
