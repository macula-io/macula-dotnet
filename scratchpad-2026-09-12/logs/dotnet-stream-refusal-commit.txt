Refuse a STREAM_OPEN not signed by its caller, drop a CALL with a wrongly typed field, and bound drop warnings

StreamHandle.AcceptAsync checks the first frame of every inbound stream
before anything else looks at it. A STREAM_OPEN whose signature doesn't
verify against the caller it names (invalid_signature), one without a
64-byte signature or a 32-byte caller (unsigned), a frame of another type
(not_a_stream_open) or bytes that don't decode (malformed) get the stream
aborted in both directions with code 2, with nothing written, and
AcceptAsync waits for the next stream within its timeout. Stream handlers
previously received the caller field unverified. A verified STREAM_OPEN
carries its caller in map args, as a CALL handler's payload does.
StreamHandle.RefuseAsync writes a STREAM_ERROR, finishes the send side and
stops reading with code 2.

An inbound CALL whose ucan_token is neither bytes, text nor null is dropped
as malformed and serving goes on; it used to end the session.

A session traces a dropped CALL, a reply for no pending call and a refused
stream with one bounded warning per kind: a line at once, and one closing
line with the rest counted when the interval ends
(Session.DropWarningInterval, 60 seconds by default). Control characters in
a procedure are escaped so a warning stays on one line. A frame that
doesn't decode ends a session with MalformedFrameException.
