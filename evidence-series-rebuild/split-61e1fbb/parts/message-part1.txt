Drop an inbound CALL whose ucan_token has the wrong type and serve the next

CallFrameParsing.ParseCall now throws ParseFrameException for a ucan_token
that is neither bytes, text nor null, so the reader drops the CALL as
malformed and serving goes on. Value.AsBytes used to throw
InvalidOperationException there, which ended the session.
