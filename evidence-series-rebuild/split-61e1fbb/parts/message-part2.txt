Trace dropped CALLs and replies as bounded drop warnings

A session traces a dropped inbound CALL and a RESULT or ERROR for no pending
call with one bounded warning per kind: a line at once, and one closing line
with the rest counted when the interval ends (Session.DropWarningInterval,
60 seconds by default). The kinds, reasons and fields follow the shared
drop warning rule: unsigned for a signature that is missing or not 64 bytes
or a caller that is not a 32-byte key, invalid_signature for one that fails,
and the procedure cut to 256 bytes with its control characters escaped so a
warning stays on one line, or the call_id's first 4 bytes. A frame that
doesn't decode ends a session with MalformedFrameException. The trace
listener the tests share moves to its own file.
