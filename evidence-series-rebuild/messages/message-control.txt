Complete a call only with a reply signed by its responder

A RESULT or ERROR on the control stream completes its call only when it is
signed by the key its responded_by or reported_by names, parses with a
call_id, and that call_id is one a call waits for, checked in that order.
Any other reply is dropped, counted as unrouted and warned about as a
dropped reply with its reason, and the call keeps waiting. A signed reply
for a waiting call that does not parse no longer fails the call.

The fake station in the tests signs its replies with its own key. The two
tests for replies that do not verify have macula-go's names.
