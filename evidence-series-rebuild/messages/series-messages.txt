==== d1f67da
Try each direct-dial provider in turn within the call's timeout

DirectDial now treats every advertisement (or content announcement) that
verifies as a candidate, in DHT order, and moves to the next one when a
candidate's endpoint lookup, dial or identity check fails before anything
is sent. A request that has gone out is never sent again. When nothing
qualifies, the DHT is queried again with a backoff of 100 ms doubling to
1 s, and within one call a station that already failed is dialed again
only once its advertisement or endpoint record has changed.

The call's timeout now bounds resolution, every endpoint lookup, every
dial and the request, with each candidate getting a share of what remains.
Resolve and ResolveStationEndpoint get a 10 s budget. GetDirect retries
while nothing is announced and moves on after a failed or unverified
fetch.

The resolution logic sits behind internal delegates over the DHT lookups,
the dial and the request, so DirectDialCandidatesTests exercises it with
real signed records and no network. The cert-chain test certificates move
to CertChainFixtures so both test classes share them.

Bumps the package to 0.5.0 and starts CHANGELOG.md.

==== ddb3e29
Bind UCAN-gated calls to the caller that signed them

A provider now answers an inbound CALL only when the CALL's signature
verifies against the caller it names. One that doesn't is dropped with no
reply, before policy or a handler runs, in both Session's serve loop and
StationPool's inbound path, matching macula_station_link.erl's
on_inbound_call/3.

Policy.Check takes the caller as well as the token. A Policy.Required
procedure accepts a token only when its aud is that caller's node id as
lowercase hex, the comparison macula_station_link.erl uses; a missing
caller or a different audience is unauthorized. UcanToken.Create's doc,
the UCAN examples and the live UCAN tests mint tokens with that audience.

ServeCallerTests and the new UcanTests cases carry the same names as
macula-go's.

==== cdff56a

==== cbbf756
Reuse an open session for stream and content direct dial

A station keeps one connection per identity and closes the older one when
a newer one completes its handshake. Direct dial opened a second
connection under the caller's identity, which closed resolveVia or a pool
link to the same station.

Sessions now register by identity and station once HELLO is accepted,
and unregister on close. OpenStreamDirectAsync,
OpenStreamDirectWithCertChainAsync, PutDirectAsync and GetDirectAsync run
on a registered session, on a dedicated QUIC stream, and leave it open.
The stream calls return DirectDial.OpenedStream, which says whether the
caller owns the session. Calls still dial their own connection: reusing a
session for a call needs a per-session reader first.

==== bec6c5b
Serialize frame sends on a FrameStream

System.Net.Quic's QuicStream fails a write that starts while another is
still pending, with InvalidOperationException. FrameStream wrote each
frame with its own WriteAsync and nothing serialized them, so two tasks
sending on one Session at once, such as a publish and the
publish_started fact RunPublisherAsync fires beside it, could fail one
of the sends.

SendFrameAsync now takes turns through a gate, on the control stream and
on dedicated streams alike, and each frame is still written whole in a
single write.

==== 1d50860
Send the publish_started fact before the publish, not beside it

==== 080b424
Report what a direct-dial call observed when it runs out of time

A procedure call whose DHT lookups all failed threw
ProcedureNotAdvertisedException, and a call whose lookups were never
answered in time threw ProcedureNotAdvertisedException,
ContentNotAnnouncedException or StationEndpointNotFoundException: an
absence nobody observed. A failed content lookup after an answered one
also replaced what the answered one found, and a station_endpoint lookup
that failed ended at once instead of being retried.

deadline records nothing. A station_endpoint lookup retries a failed
lookup within its budget and throws StationEndpointNotFoundException
only when a lookup was answered, else the lookup's exception, else a
TimeoutException, and that is what a candidate's failure carries.

==== 99372c6
Share a dialed session between the direct-dial requests that reuse it

A session direct dial dialed for one request was registered for reuse,
so a later stream or transfer to the same station ran on it, and the
first request then closed it underneath the second when it finished.

A dialed session now carries a lease count. Every request that reuses it
takes a lease and releases its own when done, and the session closes
when the last lease is released; a request that finds the last lease
already released dials afresh. The session is marked as dialed before it
becomes findable, so no reuse misses its lease. OpenedStream holds its
lease until it is disposed and loses OwnsSession. Sessions the
application opened are still reused without a lease and never closed by
direct dial.

==== f1f0c35
Read each session's control stream with one reader that routes every frame

Calls, subscriptions and serving now run at the same time on one Session. The reader hands a RESULT or ERROR to its call by call_id, an EVENT to each matching Subscription (256 queued), and a CALL signed by its caller to a queue of 64. GOODBYE, or HELLO or CONNECT after the handshake, ends the session and fails waiting calls. Other frames are counted per type and traced at most once a minute.

Session.SubscribeAsync returns a Subscription with its own RecvEventAsync. Session.RecvAsync, RecvEventAsync and UnsubscribeAsync are gone. StationPool drops its pump, pending-call map and send gate. A session whose control stream ends unregisters itself from direct-dial reuse.

==== 4bb2283
Bound every write on a session and keep the reader clear of writes

Writes on the control stream take turns. A call waits for its turn within its own timeout and every other send within a 30 s send timeout; when that runs out only that caller times out, and the frame was never sent. A write that stalls past 30 s ends the session with SendTimeoutException. A call that times out throws CallTimeoutException, whose WriteStarted says whether the CALL may have reached the station.

The reader never waits on a write: temporary_relay_failure for an overflowing inbound CALL and the RPC telemetry facts go through a hand-off of 64 and are dropped when it is full, with rpc.sent_v1 handed over once the CALL is written. An overflowing call queue no longer ends serving. Subscribing and closing take turns across the count change and the frame, so SUBSCRIBE and UNSUBSCRIBE reach the station in order, and an overflowed subscription keeps the station subscribed until it is closed. The pool's link calls and its overflow consumer run on the same paths. Test names match macula-go.

==== 7ec12f4
End a session cleanly and retry pool calls only when they were not sent

An operation on a session that has ended throws SessionEndedException, whose InnerException is the reason and whose WriteStarted says whether a call's CALL may have reached the station. A call waiting for its turn to write returns promptly when the session ends, and a frame that can't be decoded ends the session. A session whose control stream ends also closes its connection now.

StationPool.CallAsync moves to the next link only when the CALL was not sent there. Pool calls publish no RPC telemetry facts, as macula's pool doesn't. The in-memory station the reader tests use moves to its own file so the pool tests share it. Test names match macula-go.

==== 61e1fbb
Refuse a STREAM_OPEN not signed by its caller, drop a CALL with a wrongly typed field, and bound drop warnings

StreamHandle.AcceptAsync checks the first frame of every inbound stream
before anything else looks at it. A STREAM_OPEN whose signature doesn't
verify against the caller it names (invalid_signature), one without a
64-byte signature or a 32-byte caller (unsigned), a frame of another type
(not_a_stream_open) or bytes that don't decode (malformed) get the stream
aborted in both directions with code 2, with nothing written, and
AcceptAsync waits for the next stream within its timeout. Stream handlers
previously received the caller field unverified. A verified STREAM_OPEN
carries its caller in map args, as a CALL handler's payload does.
StreamHandle.RefuseAsync writes a STREAM_ERROR, finishes the send side and
stops reading with code 2.

An inbound CALL whose ucan_token is neither bytes, text nor null is dropped
as malformed and serving goes on; it used to end the session.

A session traces a dropped CALL, a reply for no pending call and a refused
stream with one bounded warning per kind: a line at once, and one closing
line with the rest counted when the interval ends
(Session.DropWarningInterval, 60 seconds by default). Control characters in
a procedure are escaped so a warning stays on one line. A frame that
doesn't decode ends a session with MalformedFrameException.
