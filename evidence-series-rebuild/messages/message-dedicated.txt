Accept a dedicated stream reply only when signed by its responder

A call on a dedicated stream, as content transfer makes, takes a RESULT or
ERROR only when it is signed by the key its responded_by or reported_by
names, parses, and carries the call's call_id, checked in that order. Any
other reply is dropped and warned about as a dropped reply on the session
the stream belongs to, and the call keeps waiting until its timeout. A
stream a session opens or accepts carries the session's drop warnings.

The two tests have macula-go's names.
